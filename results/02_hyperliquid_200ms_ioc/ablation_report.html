Calibration context (public documentation, retrieved 2026-11-18; not simulation inputs)

  • Hyperliquid: end-to-end latency: The docs define end-to-end latency as the time from sending a request to receiving the committed response. A third-party mirror that cites the official docs reports a median of about 0.2 s and a 99th percentile of about 0.9 s for a co-located client (an older analyst note quotes a 0.3 s median). The numeric values were not visible in the official page excerpt retrieved here. Source: https://hyperliquid.gitbook.io/hyperliquid-docs/hypercore/overview ; https://www.dwellir.com/docs/hyperliquid/architecture
  • Hyperliquid: block interval: Not stated in the official page retrieved. A third-party node-docs site says blocks come about every 70 ms with finality in about two rounds (around 140 ms); an analyst post describes block commits in the range of 200-300 ms and an observed lag of about 700 ms in fills versus Binance (maker update in one block, taker fill in the next). Treat the block interval as roughly 70-300 ms and UNVERIFIED. Source: https://hypernode-docs.polynode.dev/research/consensus ; https://arrakis.finance/blog/crypto-price-discovery
  • dYdX Chain (v4): block time: The official onboarding FAQ states an average block time of about 1 second, and that short-term order cancels are best effort until the block is final. Some third-party pages claim 0.5 s or even 100 ms blocks; they conflict with the official FAQ and are not used here. Source: https://docs.dydx.xyz/concepts/onboarding-faqs
  • Not documented (stay assumptions): No venue documentation was found for market-maker reaction time, market-data feed latency, client-to-sequencer network latency or latency-arbitrageur reaction time. The model defaults (feed 5 ms, MM reaction 10 ms, sniper reaction 2 ms, network 30 ms) are assumptions. Sweep them (--mm-react) instead of trusting a single value. Source: n/a
  • Dywe window: The 50 ms micro-batch and the 35 ms decryption RTT are Dywe design/benchmark parameters, not venue documentation. They are not validated by any source above. Source: n/a
  • Implication for Regime B: If B is meant to represent Hyperliquid, a 50 ms window is shorter than every documented latency figure above. Use --b-window-ms 70 and --b-window-ms 200 (separate runs) to read the cancel-priority gap at those scales, and --b-window-ms 1000 for a dYdX-like cadence. Source: n/a
