Methodological Scope & Statistical Boundaries

  • Regime D Execution Timeline: Orders arrive at arr_ns inside [batch_start, batch_close), commit at batch_close, complete threshold decryption at clear_ns = batch_close + decrypt_ms, and clear atomically against the post-cancel book.
  • Sniper Attribution & Cohort Separation: StaleSnipe tracks orders hitting the un-updated quote ID targeted at trigger time (proc.id == target_id); LateSnipe tracks sniper orders arriving after MM already requoted to a fresh reference mid.
  • 95% Bootstrap Confidence Intervals: Computed via joint 3-hour block resampling across BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT (56 blocks). CI reflects empirical drift variance under fixed scenario assumptions and does not cover parameter uncertainty.
  • Threshold Encryption Scope: In Regime D, BLS12-381 threshold encryption protects order payloads (side, price, size) while Cancel-Header messages remain plaintext for head-of-batch priority (modeled conservatively with 10% metadata leakage in the tables above).
